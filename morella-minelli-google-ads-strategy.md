# Morella Minelli — Google Ads Strategy
## Four 30-Day Campaign Plan

---

## Brand Overview

**Client:** Morella Minelli
**Category:** Fashion & Lifestyle
**Campaign Duration:** 4 × 30-Day Phases (120 days total)
**Ad Platform:** Google Ads (Search, Display, Shopping, YouTube)

---

| Phase | Days | Theme | Primary Goal | Primary Channel |
|-------|------|--------|-------------|-----------------|

## Phase 1 — Foundation & Discovery (Days 1–30)

### Objective
Introduce Morella Minelli to cold audiences and build brand recognition. Establish baseline data for all future optimization.

### Campaigns

#### 1A — Brand Awareness Display Campaign
- **Type:** Google Display Network (GDN)
- **Bidding:** Target CPM (maximize impressions)
- **Audience Targeting:**
  - In-market: Women's Apparel, Luxury Fashion, Designer Accessories
  - Affinity: Fashion Enthusiasts, Frequent Shoppers
  - Demographic: Women 25–45, HHI top 30%
- **Ad Formats:** Responsive Display Ads (RDA) — hero lifestyle imagery, 3 headlines, 2 descriptions
- **Budget Allocation:** 35% of monthly budget
- **KPIs:** Impressions, Reach, Brand Search Lift, View-Through Conversions

#### 1B — Branded Search Campaign
- **Type:** Search
- **Bidding:** Manual CPC (protect brand terms)
- **Keywords:**
  - Exact: [morella minelli], [morella minelli fashion], [morella minelli store]
  - Broad Match Modifier: +morella +minelli
- **Ad Copy Theme:** Establish brand promise — "Timeless Italian Style. Morella Minelli."
- **Budget Allocation:** 10% of monthly budget
- **KPIs:** Impression Share (target >95%), CTR, Brand Query Volume

#### 1C — Non-Brand Discovery Search Campaign
- **Type:** Search
- **Bidding:** Maximize Clicks (with CPC cap)
- **Keywords (broad intent):**
  - "italian fashion brand"
  - "luxury women's clothing"
  - "designer dress boutique"
  - "elegant women's fashion online"
- **Budget Allocation:** 30% of monthly budget
- **KPIs:** CTR, New Users, Bounce Rate, Pages per Session

#### 1D — YouTube Awareness (Optional but Recommended)
- **Type:** YouTube — Skippable In-Stream
- **Bidding:** Target CPV
- **Creative:** 30-second brand story video
- **Audience:** Similar to 1A affinity audiences
- **Budget Allocation:** 25% of monthly budget
- **KPIs:** View Rate, Brand Lift Survey, Earned Actions

### Phase 1 Milestones
- [ ] Conversion tracking verified (purchases, add-to-cart, lead forms)
- [ ] Google Merchant Center feed live and approved
- [ ] Audience lists building (Remarketing, Customer Match)
- [ ] Baseline CTR and CPC benchmarks established
- [ ] Search term report reviewed — negatives added

## Phase 2 — Desire & Engagement (Days 31–60)

### Objective
Drive qualified traffic deep into the site. Warm up remarketing audiences. Push high-intent searchers toward product pages.

### Campaigns

#### 2A — Non-Brand Search (Refined)
- **Type:** Search
- **Bidding:** Maximize Conversions (switch from Phase 1 once 30+ conversions collected)
- **Optimizations from Phase 1:**
  - Pause underperforming ad groups (CTR < 1%)
  - Promote top performers to exact/phrase match
  - Add 20+ new negatives from Search Terms Report
- **New Ad Groups:** Category-level targeting
  - "shop [dresses / tops / accessories] online"
  - "boutique fashion [category] women"
- **Budget Allocation:** 35% of monthly budget

#### 2B — Google Shopping Campaign
- **Type:** Standard Shopping
- **Bidding:** Enhanced CPC → Target ROAS (if data permits)
- **Feed Optimization:**
  - Product titles: Brand + Category + Key Attribute + Color
  - Custom labels: Margin tier (high / mid / low), Best Sellers, New Arrivals
- **Campaign Structure:** Product category segmentation (Dresses, Tops, Accessories, etc.)
- **Budget Allocation:** 30% of monthly budget
- **KPIs:** ROAS, CTR, Impression Share, Cart Abandonment Rate

#### 2C — Remarketing Display Campaign
- **Type:** GDN Remarketing
- **Bidding:** Target CPA
- **Audience Segments:**
  - Site visitors (last 30 days) — exclude converters
  - Product page viewers (last 14 days) — high intent
  - Add-to-cart abandoners (last 7 days) — highest intent
- **Ad Sequence:** 3-touch sequence
  1. Touch 1: Lifestyle image ("You were looking at…")
  2. Touch 2: Product-specific creative + social proof
  3. Touch 3: Urgency/offer ("Limited stock — complete your look")
- **Budget Allocation:** 20% of monthly budget
- **KPIs:** View-Through Conversions, Return Visit Rate, CPA

#### 2D — Branded Search (Maintained)
- **Budget Allocation:** 15% of monthly budget
- **Updates:** Add new sitelink extensions, callout extensions, structured snippets

### Phase 2 Milestones
- [ ] Shopping feed achieving >90% impression share on top products
- [ ] Remarketing audiences populated (min. 100 users each)
- [ ] At least 2 ad copy variants A/B tested per ad group
- [ ] Negative keyword list expanded to 50+ terms
- [ ] Landing pages reviewed for mobile speed (target <3s load)

## Phase 3 — Conversion & Sales (Days 61–90)

### Objective
Maximize ROAS. Close warm audiences. Introduce Performance Max to discover new converting segments.

### Campaigns

#### 3A — Performance Max (PMax)
- **Type:** Performance Max
- **Bidding:** Target ROAS (set 15–20% below actual observed ROAS to give learning room)
- **Asset Groups:**
  - Group 1: New Arrivals (latest season imagery, urgency copy)
  - Group 2: Best Sellers (social proof copy, ratings)
  - Group 3: Promotional (if applicable — sale items, bundles)
- **Audience Signals:**
  - Customer Match list (existing customers)
  - Remarketing lists from Phase 1–2
  - Custom intent audiences (competitor brand terms, luxury fashion searches)
- **Budget Allocation:** 35% of monthly budget
- **KPIs:** Conv. Value / Cost (ROAS), New Customer Acquisition Rate, Asset Group performance

#### 3B — Shopping (Scaled)
- **Type:** Smart Bidding — Target ROAS
- **Focus:** Top 20% of SKUs by revenue contribution (Pareto principle)
- **New Structure:** Priority campaign layering
  - High priority: Best Sellers with tight product filters
  - Medium priority: Category campaigns
  - Low priority: Catch-all
- **Budget Allocation:** 30% of monthly budget

#### 3C — Search (Conversion-Focused)
- **Type:** Search — Target CPA
- **Ad Groups:** Only retain ad groups with >5 conversions
- **RSA Optimization:** Pin best-performing headlines in position 1 and 2
- **Budget Allocation:** 20% of monthly budget

#### 3D — Cart Abandonment Remarketing (Intensified)
- **Audience:** Add-to-cart, checkout initiated (last 7 days)
- **Bidding:** Target CPA (aggressive)
- **Ad Copy:** Direct response — "Your cart is waiting. Free shipping today only."
- **Budget Allocation:** 15% of monthly budget
- **KPIs:** Recovery Rate, CPA, Revenue Recovered

### Phase 3 Milestones
- [ ] PMax campaign out of learning phase (min. 50 conversions)
- [ ] ROAS trending positive week-over-week
- [ ] Top 3 revenue-generating ad groups identified and budget-shifted toward
- [ ] Auction insights report reviewed — competitor share analyzed
- [ ] Product feed promotions activated for high-margin SKUs

## Phase 4 — Loyalty & Scale (Days 91–120)

### Objective
Re-engage existing customers. Scale what's working. Build sustainable channel economics for ongoing operations.

### Campaigns

#### 4A — Customer Loyalty Search
- **Type:** Search
- **Audience Layer:** Customer Match (past purchasers) overlaid on non-brand keywords
- **Bidding:** Bid adjustment +20% for known customers
- **Ad Copy Theme:** VIP / returning customer messaging — "Welcome back. New season arrivals."
- **Budget Allocation:** 20% of monthly budget

#### 4B — Performance Max (Optimized)
- **Action:** Consolidate learnings from Phase 3
  - Delete underperforming asset groups
  - Add new creative assets based on top performers
  - Refresh audience signals with updated customer lists
- **New Asset Group:** Seasonal or promotional (if applicable)
- **Budget Allocation:** 35% of monthly budget

#### 4C — Dynamic Search Ads (DSA)
- **Type:** Search — Dynamic
- **Target:** Auto-targets site pages not covered by existing keyword campaigns
- **Good for:** Capturing long-tail, editorial, and blog traffic
- **Budget Allocation:** 10% of monthly budget

#### 4D — YouTube Retargeting
- **Type:** YouTube — In-Stream
- **Audience:** Phase 1–3 site visitors who have not converted
- **Creative:** Testimonial-style video, 15–30 seconds
- **Bidding:** Target CPV
- **Budget Allocation:** 20% of monthly budget

#### 4E — Branded (Maintained)
- **Budget Allocation:** 15% of monthly budget
- **New Extensions:** Promotion extensions, Price extensions for key categories

### Phase 4 Milestones
- [ ] Full-funnel attribution report produced (first-click, last-click, data-driven)
- [ ] Customer LTV analyzed — identify top customer segments for lookalike expansion
- [ ] Month 4 ROAS vs. Month 1 baseline comparison documented
- [ ] Evergreen playbook written for ongoing campaign management
- [ ] Recommendations for Month 5+ budget allocation delivered

## Weekly Optimization Cadence

**Every Monday:**
- Review Search Terms Report — add negatives
- Check budget pacing — adjust daily budgets if needed
- Review Quality Scores — address <5 QS ad groups

**Every Wednesday:**
- Bid adjustments based on device, location, time-of-day data
- A/B test results review — pause losers
- Landing page performance check (bounce rate, conversion rate)

**End of Month (Before Phase Transition):**
- Full performance audit vs. KPI targets
- Audience list refresh
- Creative refresh (new headlines, descriptions, images)
- Competitor auction insights review
- Budget reallocation recommendation for next phase
