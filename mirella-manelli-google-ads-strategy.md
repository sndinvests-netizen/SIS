## Phase 1 — Awareness & List Building (Days 1–30)

### Objective
Put Mirella Manelli in front of hairstylists who don't yet know the brand. Establish tracking, build remarketing audiences, and benchmark baseline performance data.

### Campaigns

#### 1A — Non-Brand Search (Discovery)
- **Type:** Search
- **Bidding:** Maximize Clicks (with CPC cap)
- **Keyword Themes:**
  - "hair education for stylists"
  - "online hair courses for hairstylists"
  - "balayage training online"
  - "hair extension certification program"
  - "how to grow my salon business"
  - "hair business coaching"
- **Match Types:** Broad Match with Smart Bidding signals; add phrase and exact after search term review
- **Budget Allocation:** 35% of monthly budget
- **KPIs:** CTR, New User Rate, Bounce Rate, Avg. Session Duration

#### 1B — Brand Search (Protection)
- **Type:** Search
- **Bidding:** Manual CPC
- **Keywords:**
  - [mirella manelli], [mirella manelli education], [hair b&b university], [manelli hair extensions]
- **Goal:** Protect brand terms from competitors bidding on them
- **Budget Allocation:** 10% of monthly budget
- **KPIs:** Impression Share (target >95%), CTR

#### 1C — Hairstylist Interest Display Campaign
- **Type:** Google Display Network (GDN)
- **Bidding:** Target CPM
- **Audience Targeting:**
  - In-market: Beauty & Personal Care, Professional Beauty Services
  - Custom Intent: People searching "hair stylist courses," "cosmetology education," "salon business tips"
  - Placement targeting: Beauty industry websites, salon trade publications
- **Ad Formats:** Responsive Display Ads — lifestyle imagery of Mirella teaching, before/after hair results
- **Budget Allocation:** 30% of monthly budget
- **KPIs:** Impressions, Reach, View-Through Conversions, New Audiences Added to Remarketing Lists

#### 1D — YouTube Awareness
- **Type:** YouTube — Skippable In-Stream
- **Bidding:** Target CPV
- **Creative Direction:** Short brand story (30s) — Mirella's journey, credibility (awards, 1,500+ stylists), transformation stories from students
- **Audience:** Hairstylists, beauty professionals, cosmetology students
- **Budget Allocation:** 25% of monthly budget
- **KPIs:** View Rate, Earned Subscribers, Brand Lift

### Phase 1 Milestones
- [ ] Conversion tracking live: course enrollments, guide purchases, extension inquiries, lead form completions
- [ ] Google Merchant Center feed approved for Manelli Hair Extensions
- [ ] Remarketing audiences building: site visitors, course page viewers, extension page viewers
- [ ] Customer Match list uploaded (existing students / customers)
- [ ] Negative keyword list seeded (see master list below)
- [ ] Baseline CTR, CPC, and session quality benchmarks documented

## Phase 2 — Education Enrollment (Days 31–60)

### Objective
Convert warm traffic into Hair B&B University enrollments and digital guide purchases. Capitalize on the brand awareness built in Phase 1.

### Campaigns

#### 2A — Hair B&B University Enrollment Search
- **Type:** Search
- **Bidding:** Maximize Conversions → Target CPA (once 30+ conversions collected)
- **Dedicated Landing Page:** Hair B&B University enrollment page
- **Keyword Themes:**
  - "online hairstylist courses"
  - "hair education platform for stylists"
  - "how to grow a salon clientele"
  - "balayage online class certification"
  - "hair extension training program"
  - "social media for hairstylists course"
- **Ad Copy Angle:** Transformation + credibility — "1,500+ Stylists Trust Hair B&B University"
- **Budget Allocation:** 30% of monthly budget
- **KPIs:** Enrollment CPA, CTR, Conversion Rate

#### 2B — Digital Guides & Mini-Courses Search
- **Type:** Search
- **Bidding:** Target CPA (lower-ticket items — faster data accumulation)
- **Ad Groups by Product:**
  - Highlight Placement Blueprint
  - Ultimate Toner Guidebook
  - Natural Highlight Placement Mini-Course
  - Upstyle in 7 Days
- **Keyword Examples:**
  - "highlight placement guide for stylists"
  - "hair toning guide for colorists"
  - "how to do upstyles tutorial"
- **Budget Allocation:** 20% of monthly budget
- **KPIs:** Revenue, ROAS, CPA per product

#### 2C — YouTube In-Stream (Education Funnel)
- **Type:** YouTube — Non-Skippable (15s) + Skippable (30–60s)
- **Audience:** Remarketing (Phase 1 site visitors) + Custom Intent (competitor searches)
- **Creative:** Course testimonial videos from enrolled stylists; demo clips from Hair B&B University
- **CTA:** "Enroll Free Today" / "Download the Blueprint"
- **Budget Allocation:** 25% of monthly budget
- **KPIs:** View Rate, Course Page Visits, Enrollment Rate

#### 2D — Remarketing Display (Education Focus)
- **Type:** GDN Remarketing
- **Bidding:** Target CPA
- **Audience Segments:**
  - University enrollment page visitors (no conversion, last 14 days)
  - Podcast page visitors (highly engaged, warm)
  - YouTube video viewers (25%+ watched)
- **Ad Creative:** Social proof ("Join 1,500+ stylists"), urgency ("Next live session starts soon")
- **Budget Allocation:** 15% of monthly budget

#### 2E — Brand Search (Maintained)
- **Budget Allocation:** 10% of monthly budget

### Phase 2 Milestones
- [ ] Enrollment CPA established and documented
- [ ] At least 2 RSA variants A/B tested per ad group
- [ ] YouTube retargeting audience >100 users
- [ ] Search Terms Report reviewed — 30+ new negatives added
- [ ] Top converting keywords promoted to exact match

## Phase 3 — Extensions & Product Sales (Days 61–90)

### Objective
Drive Manelli Hair Extensions purchases (B2B: salon professionals; B2C: consumers). Close remaining warm audiences on digital products. Introduce Performance Max.

### Campaigns

#### 3A — Manelli Hair Extensions Shopping Campaign
- **Type:** Standard Shopping → Smart Shopping (once ROAS data available)
- **Merchant Center Feed Optimization:**
  - Titles: "Manelli [Product Type] | 100% Remy Human Hair | [Length/Color]"
  - Custom Labels: Best Seller, New, High Margin, Certification Bundle
- **Bidding:** Enhanced CPC → Target ROAS
- **Campaign Structure:**
  - High Priority: Best-selling SKUs
  - Medium Priority: Product type segmentation (Weft, Tape-in, etc.)
  - Low Priority: Catch-all
- **Budget Allocation:** 30% of monthly budget
- **KPIs:** ROAS, Revenue, Impression Share

#### 3B — Extensions Certification Search
- **Type:** Search
- **Bidding:** Target CPA
- **Keyword Themes:**
  - "hair extension certification near me"
  - "become a certified extension stylist"
  - "weft hair extension training"
  - "Manelli certified stylist program"
- **Ad Copy:** "Become Manelli-Certified — In-Person & Virtual Training Available"
- **Budget Allocation:** 20% of monthly budget

#### 3C — Performance Max (Launch)
- **Type:** Performance Max
- **Asset Groups:**
  - Group 1: Hair B&B University — education funnel
  - Group 2: Manelli Hair Extensions — product purchase
  - Group 3: In-Person Classes / Education Tour — event registrations
- **Audience Signals:**
  - Customer Match (all past purchasers + enrolled students)
  - Remarketing (Phase 1–2 full site visitors)
  - Custom Intent: Competitor brand searches (e.g., "Great Lengths extensions," "Bellami hair education")
- **Bidding:** Target ROAS (set 20% below observed ROAS to allow learning)
- **Budget Allocation:** 35% of monthly budget
- **KPIs:** Conv. Value / Cost, New Customer Rate, Asset Group Performance

#### 3D — Amazon DSP / Search Ads (Optional)
- **If using Amazon Advertising:** Sponsored Products on "Hair B&B" book, Sponsored Brands for Mirella Manelli storefront
- **Goal:** Capture high-purchase-intent hair tool searches

#### 3E — Brand Search (Maintained)
- **Budget Allocation:** 15% of monthly budget

### Phase 3 Milestones
- [ ] Shopping feed achieving >80% impression share on top 10 SKUs
- [ ] PMax out of learning phase (min. 50 conversions across asset groups)
- [ ] Extension certification CPA established
- [ ] Auction insights reviewed — identify key competitors
- [ ] ROAS trending positively week-over-week

## Phase 4 — Community Growth & Scale (Days 91–120)

### Objective
Re-engage existing students and customers with upsell offers. Drive registrations for in-person Education Tour events. Build a sustainable evergreen campaign structure.

### Campaigns

#### 4A — In-Person Education Tour Search
- **Type:** Search
- **Bidding:** Target CPA (registration as conversion)
- **Keyword Themes:**
  - "hair class near me for stylists"
  - "in-person balayage class [city]"
  - "hair extension hands-on training [city]"
  - "hairstylist workshop 2026"
- **Geo-Targeting:** Tour city locations (radius targeting around event venues)
- **Ad Copy:** "HairB&B Education Tour — Register Now | Limited Seats"
- **Budget Allocation:** 25% of monthly budget

#### 4B — Performance Max (Optimized & Scaled)
- **Actions:**
  - Refresh creative assets with best performers from Phases 1–3
  - Update audience signals with new customer lists
  - Add seasonal / promotional asset groups if applicable
  - Increase budget toward top-converting asset groups
- **Budget Allocation:** 35% of monthly budget

#### 4C — Customer Loyalty & Upsell Search
- **Type:** Search
- **Audience Layer:** Customer Match (past buyers) overlaid on course/extensions keywords
- **Bid Adjustment:** +25% for known customers
- **Ad Copy:** Return/VIP messaging — "You're a Hair B&B student — here's what's new"
- **Budget Allocation:** 15% of monthly budget

#### 4D — Dynamic Search Ads (DSA)
- **Type:** Search — Dynamic
- **Target:** Blog, podcast, and resource pages — catches long-tail, editorial queries
- **Budget Allocation:** 10% of monthly budget

#### 4E — YouTube Retargeting (Conversion Push)
- **Type:** YouTube — In-Stream (15s)
- **Audience:** All Phase 1–3 visitors who never converted
- **Creative:** Testimonial-driven — real stylist transformation stories
- **CTA:** Final push to enroll / shop
- **Budget Allocation:** 15% of monthly budget

### Phase 4 Milestones
- [ ] In-person event registrations tracked and reported per tour city
- [ ] Full 120-day attribution analysis: first-click vs. last-click vs. data-driven
- [ ] Student LTV segmentation — identify highest-value cohort for lookalike expansion
- [ ] Month 4 ROAS vs. Month 1 baseline comparison documented
- [ ] Evergreen campaign playbook drafted for ongoing management
- [ ] Month 5+ budget and channel allocation recommendation delivered

## Weekly Optimization Cadence

**Every Monday:**
- Search Terms Report — mine for new negatives and positive keyword candidates
- Budget pacing check — adjust daily budgets if overspending/underspending
- Quality Score audit — address ad groups with QS < 5

**Every Wednesday:**
- Bid adjustments: device, location, hour-of-day, audience
- A/B test results — pause losing RSA variants, promote winners
- Landing page performance: bounce rate, conversion rate, mobile speed

**End of Month (Before Phase Transition):**
- Full KPI audit vs. targets
- Audience list refresh (Customer Match re-upload, remarketing review)
- Creative refresh — new headlines, descriptions, imagery
- Competitor auction insights review
- Budget reallocation brief for next phase
